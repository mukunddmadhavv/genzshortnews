Why is Justice for Mau trending around Flying Beast?

Gaurav Taneja's Labrador, Mau, has died, and questions about his care have sparked a major online controversy.

Mau had moved from the family home to a farmhouse. Taneja had previously cited lifestyle changes and religious reasons for that decision.

Former employee Pushpendra Choudhary then alleged that Mau wasn't getting proper food or timely medical attention. His posts raised questions about the dog's living conditions and final days.

Now, Taneja has responded. He denies neglect and says Mau received treatment at five hospitals, with several people involved in his care.

He also says he has medical records, and disputes the former employee's account.

The backlash has even led to boycott calls against his businesses.

But here's the important distinction: these are disputed claims. The Indian Express reports that the public information does not establish Mau's cause of death or prove that Taneja caused it.

The key questions are about the care Mau received, and what the records show. Follow for updates with context.
